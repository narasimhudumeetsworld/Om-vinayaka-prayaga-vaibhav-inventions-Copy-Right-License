## Legal Framework for Media Coverage

### ✅ PERMITTED Media Activities

#### News Coverage and Reporting
- **General descriptions** of inventions and innovations for informational purposes
- **News reporting** about the inventor, portfolio, and general innovation scope
- **Educational coverage** about intellectual property protection frameworks
- **Industry analysis** mentioning the innovation portfolio in broader context

#### Attribution and Referencing
- **Proper attribution** to Prayaga Vaibhav as inventor and copyright holder
- **Linking** to this repository for complete licensing information
- **Citation** in academic or professional contexts with full attribution
- **Reference** in industry reports with proper acknowledgment

### ❌ PROHIBITED Media Activities

#### Detailed Technical Disclosure
- **Reproduction** of actual invention details, designs, or implementations
- **Technical specifications** or detailed operational descriptions
- **Diagrams, schematics,** or visual representations of inventions
- **Source code, algorithms,** or proprietary methodologies

#### Unauthorized Usage
- **Commercial use** of invention information or media coverage
- **Claiming usage rights** through media coverage or reporting
- **Derivative content** based on protected intellectual property
- **Unauthorized sharing** without proper attribution and permissions

## Media Contact Procedures

### For Standard News Coverage
1. **Review these guidelines** thoroughly before coverage
2. **Use provided attribution templates** for proper credit
3. **Focus on general descriptions** rather than technical details
4. **Include required attribution** in all published content

### For Detailed Coverage Requests
1. **Submit written request** to official contact channels
2. **Specify intended scope** and level of technical detail
3. **Wait for explicit permission** before proceeding with detailed coverage
4. **Follow any additional requirements** specified in the permission

### For Interview Requests
1. **Contact directly** using provided contact information
2. **Specify interview format** (written, audio, video, live)
3. **Provide questions in advance** for review and approval
4. **Agree to attribution requirements** before scheduling

## Content Guidelines by Media Type

### Print and Digital Articles
- Include attribution in article byline or footer
- Link to license repository in digital versions
- Use general descriptions rather than technical specifications
- Include "All Rights Reserved" notice prominently

### Broadcast Media (TV/Radio)
- Include verbal attribution during coverage
- Display license repository URL if visual medium
- Mention "All Rights Reserved" status clearly
- Provide contact information for follow-up

## Legal Compliance and Risk Management

### Compliance Checklist
Before publishing any coverage:

- [ ] Attribution requirements fulfilled completely
- [ ] No detailed technical disclosure included
- [ ] "All Rights Reserved" notice included
- [ ] Repository link provided for licensing information
- [ ] Contact information included for permissions
- [ ] General descriptions only (no specific implementations)
- [ ] "News purposes only" disclaimer included

### Risk Mitigation
- **Review content** with legal team before publication
- **Document permission requests** and approvals
- **Maintain attribution standards** consistently
- **Avoid technical speculation** about implementations
- **Respect intellectual property boundaries** at all times
