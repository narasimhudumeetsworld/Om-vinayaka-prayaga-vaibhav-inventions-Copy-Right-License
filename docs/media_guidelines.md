### ❌ PROHIBITED Media Activities

#### Detailed Technical Disclosure (RESTRICTED)

- **Claiming usage rights** through media coverage or reporting
- **Derivative content** based on protected intellectual property
- **Unauthorized sharing** without proper attribution and permissions
