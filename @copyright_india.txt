AUTOMATIC COPYRIGHT PROTECTION UNDER INDIAN LAW
==============================================

Under Section 13 of the Copyright Act, 1957, copyright protection is automatic and subsists in:
- Original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works
- Cinematograph films
- Sound recordings

Duration of Copyright (Indian Law):
- Literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work: Life of author + 60 years
- Cinematograph films and sound recordings: 60 years from publication
- Computer programmes and databases: Life of author + 60 years

LEGAL JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT
=================================

Competent Courts:
- District Court (Civil matters)
- Sessions Court (Criminal matters)
- High Court (Appeals and original jurisdiction)
- Supreme Court of India (Final appeal)

Civil Remedies (Section 55):
- Injunctions (temporary and permanent)
- Damages or account of profits
- Delivery up of infringing copies
- Anton Piller orders for search and seizure

Criminal Penalties (Section 63):
- Imprisonment: Minimum 6 months, maximum 3 years
- Fine: Minimum ₹50,000, maximum ₹2,00,000
- Both imprisonment and fine may be awarded
