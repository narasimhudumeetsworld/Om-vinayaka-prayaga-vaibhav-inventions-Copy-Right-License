## Implementation Guide

### Copy-Paste Instructions

1. **Choose the appropriate template** from the sections above based on your publication type
2. **Copy the entire text** including formatting
3. **Replace placeholders** like `[Title]` with your actual content
4. **Paste directly** into your document
5. **Verify all links** are working correctly
6. **Maintain formatting** including the Om Vinayaka attribution

### Customization Guidelines

- ✅ **Always include**: Copyright notice, "All Rights Reserved", license link, Om Vinayaka attribution
- ✅ **Maintain**: Contact information and ORCID link
- ✅ **Keep**: Reference to automatic copyright protection
- ❌ **Never remove**: Any part of the copyright notice
- ❌ **Never modify**: License links or legal language

### Legal Requirements

All templates ensure compliance with:
- Indian Copyright Act, 1957
- International Copyright Treaties (Berne Convention)
- WIPO Copyright Treaty provisions
- Automatic protection framework requirements
